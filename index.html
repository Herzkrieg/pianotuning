Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my piano be tuned?

Most manufacturers recommend tuning twice a year. At minimum, once a year keeps your piano healthy. New pianos and pianos in rooms with big humidity swings may need more frequent visits.

How long does a tuning take?

A standard tuning takes about 60–90 minutes. If your piano hasn't been serviced in a while, a pitch raise may be needed first, adding another 45–60 minutes.

My piano hasn't been tuned in years. Is it too late?

Almost never! Pianos that have gone years without service usually need a pitch raise followed by a fine tuning. We'll assess it for free and give you an honest recommendation.

Do you service digital or hybrid pianos?

We service hybrid pianos with real actions (like the Yamaha AvantGrand). Fully digital keyboards don't require tuning, but we're happy to advise on setup and maintenance.

What areas do you serve?

We cover the entire metro area and surrounding suburbs within a 40-mile radius. A small travel fee may apply for locations beyond 25 miles — we'll always tell you upfront.
